**UI/UX Job Market Analysis: Top 15 Keywords**

**Project Overview**

This project analyzes 1000+ UI/UX job postings to uncover the most frequently used skills and keywords. Unlike generic data scraping projects, this analysis focuses on deriving actionable insights about the current demand in the UI/UX job market.

**Objective**

- Identify the top 15 most commonly used keywords in UI/UX job listings.
- Understand trends in skills, tools, and responsibilities that are highly sought after by employers.
- Present the findings through data visualization to reveal patterns and insights.

**Dataset**

-Source: 1000+ publicly listed UI/UX jobs
-Key fields analyzed: Job Title, Skills/Keywords
-Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:
-Merged multiple keyword columns into a single column
-Standardized text (lowercase, removed punctuation, trimmed whitespace)
-Removed duplicates to ensure accuracy in keyword counting

**Methodology**
-Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
-Consolidated keywords into one column
-Standardized texts for uniformity
-Removed empty or null values

**Keyword Analysis**
-Counted occurrences of each unique keyword
-Ranked keywords based on frequency
